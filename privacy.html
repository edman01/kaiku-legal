Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Kaiku is provided by the Kaiku developer. For privacy questions, contact edman.build@gmail.com.

Your audio stays on your device

Kaiku records, imports and processes audio on your iPhone. Recordings, imported copies, saved effects and library information are stored in the app's local storage. We do not upload your audio to our servers or send it to Google. There is no Kaiku account or developer-operated audio server.

Kaiku asks for microphone access to record. You choose which files to import and whether to export or share a result. A file provider or app you choose may handle that file under its own privacy policy. Your device's backup settings may include Kaiku data in an Apple device backup; Kaiku does not provide its own cloud sync.

Advertising, when enabled

Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob. Kaiku requests child-directed, nonpersonalized ads suitable for a general audience, disables the publisher first-party identifier, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Google's consent service is checked before ad requests are allowed.

Nonpersonalized does not mean no data processing. Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, basic device and app information, service or app identifiers, diagnostics, ad impressions and interactions to deliver, secure and measure ads. These advertising services do not receive your audio from Kaiku. We do not add a separate analytics service. See Google's SDK data disclosure, ad treatment settings and Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, an “Ad privacy choices” control is available through an adult gate. Once a verified Remove Ads purchase is active, Kaiku stops requesting new ads. This does not erase data Google already received. Versions with advertising disabled do not initialize the ad service.

Purchases

Apple handles in-app payments. Kaiku uses verified StoreKit transaction information to check and restore the Remove Ads purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles payment records under its App Store privacy information and Privacy Policy.

Children and parents

Young users should have a parent or guardian's help with permissions, sharing, purchases and privacy choices. Kaiku applies the same child-directed advertising restrictions to every user. Its adult arithmetic gate is an access control; it is not age verification or verified parental consent to collect personal information.

Your controls and retention

You can delete saved sounds in “My sounds,” revoke microphone access in iPhone Settings, or delete the app and its local data. Deleting a Kaiku copy does not delete the original imported file, exported copies, or existing backups. Manage those through the relevant app or your Apple backup settings. Temporary audio previews may be cleared by Kaiku or iOS.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send. Our mailbox is hosted by Google. We use that information to answer your request and keep it only as needed for support and legal obligations. We cannot retrieve audio that remains only on your device.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us about information we hold. For information handled by Apple or Google, their policies explain their rights tools, retention and international processing.

This website

These pages have no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies set by us, or external fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as explained in its Pages documentation and Privacy Statement. Links you choose to open lead to services with their own policies.

Changes

We will update this page when Kaiku's privacy practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be brought to your attention as required by applicable law.
